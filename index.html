Your answer
It plays a short phrase from your box. Close your eyes, find it on the guitar, and play it back. You'll hear a chime when you get it. Every 3 in a row adds a note.

1 · Own the box

Click the notes and learn the shape with labels on "Finger". Then switch labels to "None" and play it eyes closed, up and down.

2 · Jam on one groove

Start the Blues groove, slow. Use 2–3 notes per phrase. Bend the ring-finger note on the G string up until it matches the next note in the box.

3 · Sweet vs bluesy

Switch the mood to Sweet. The shape is the same, but your hand moves down 3 frets. That bright sound is the ballad side.

4 · Land on the ring

End each phrase on a ringed note (a note of the current chord). That's the difference between noodling and sounding like you meant it.

5 · Echo every day

Five minutes of the Echo game, eyes closed. This builds the ear-to-hand link that lets you pick up and play.

6 · Play along to real songs

Use "Find a song's key" with songs you love, then jam over them. Hum a line first, then find it in the box.

Works best in Chrome or Edge. The mic stays on your device; nothing is recorded or uploaded.
